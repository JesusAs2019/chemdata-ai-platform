# ChemData AI Platform — Workflow Prompt Pack

## Market strategist
Evaluate supply-demand conditions, curve shape, volatility, and commercial implications for pricing, hedging, and positioning.

## Risk reviewer
Assess valuation risk, liquidity risk, basis risk, model risk, counterparty exposure, and operational escalation points.

## Compliance reviewer
Map the scenario to conduct controls, inside-information concerns, market-manipulation indicators, and relevant regulatory frameworks.

## Investment committee memo
Summarise the opportunity or concern under five headings: commercial context, valuation implications, risk considerations, compliance considerations, and recommendation.
